BitcoinQuantum — Global Forensic Audit and Digital Asset Ownership Verification

An Open Declaration from Malaysia to the World

Proof Before Trust. Verification Before Recognition.

I am issuing this public declaration from Malaysia to request an independent international forensic examination of digital asset records, blockchain transactions, and ownership claims.

I have previously sought guidance from Malaysian institutions, including the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (LHDN), Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM), and the Securities Commission Malaysia (SC). I am now seeking a clear, lawful, and transparent process through which relevant evidence can be independently examined.

I invite governments, qualified blockchain researchers, cryptographers, forensic auditors, legal professionals, academic institutions, and competent regulatory authorities to participate in this examination.

Through the BitcoinQuantum initiative, my objectives are to:

- Verify blockchain transactions, wallet addresses, and digital asset records.
- Preserve evidence integrity using SHA-256.
- Examine valid cryptographic proof of wallet control.
- Distinguish blockchain activity from legal ownership.
- Seek independent forensic review and lawful ownership determination.
- Support transparency, accountability, and international cooperation.

I do not ask the world to accept my ownership claims without proof. I ask the world to examine the evidence.

All findings must remain open to independent verification, correction, and challenge.

If any assets are ultimately established through lawful procedures to belong to me, I hope to explore opportunities to contribute to education, economic development, public welfare, and humanitarian progress in Malaysia and internationally.

No private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or confidential financial information should be publicly disclosed as part of this process.

Current status: Ownership claims are subject to independent verification and legal determination. No final determination is asserted.

A Message to the World

Let evidence speak.

Let independent experts examine the records.

Let the law determine ownership.

Let transparency serve humanity.
